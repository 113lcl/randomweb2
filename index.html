01 / Philosophy

Architecture is what remains
once everything unnecessary is removed

01
Villa Meridian
French Riviera, 2023

Every project begins with one question: what does a person truly need in this space? We remove everything that stands in the way of light, air and quiet — and keep only what matters.

01

Minimal form, maximum meaning

Every line is justified by light, climate and the way a person moves through the space.

02

Materials speak for themselves

Stone, glass and timber — unpainted, unimitated. Only the honest texture of time.

03

Engineering stays invisible

Climate, light and security systems dissolve into the architecture rather than interrupt it.

“The best detail is the one you never notice, but feel every single day.” — Nicolas Verane, Founder

03 / Capabilities

What's included in every project

Full-cycle delivery

From concept and permitting to final decor — one team carries the project from idea to keys.

Bespoke material studio

Our own stone and timber workshops in Italy allow us to create pieces that exist nowhere else.

Climate & light engineering

Adaptive systems that respond to season, time of day and human presence within a room.

Privacy & security

Designed around complete owner confidentiality — from the plot boundary to the home's digital systems.

Aftercare, always

A personal home curator stays in touch for seasonal maintenance and evolving needs.

Landscape integration

Each home is designed as a continuation of the terrain — never an object placed on top of it.
