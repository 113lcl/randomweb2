Stone Loft

A nineteenth-century warehouse floor reworked from the inside, without touching the shell that made it worth keeping.

Location
Milan
Completed
2023
Area
210 m²
Materials
Travertine, steel, oak
Duration
8 months
The brief

Interiors only — the shell was already right

Not every project needs a new building. Stone Loft is an interiors-only commission inside a former textile warehouse whose cast-iron columns and eight-metre ceilings were the entire reason the client bought it — our job was to add a life to the space without erasing the reasons it was worth saving.

A single travertine volume, waist-high, houses the kitchen and both bathrooms without ever touching the original brick perimeter walls. Everything that needed to be added — plumbing, wiring, structure for a mezzanine bedroom — lives inside that one inserted block, leaving the historic shell completely legible and, if a future owner wanted, fully reversible.

Blackened steel stitches the new mezzanine to the original columns using the same rivet spacing as the warehouse's original ironwork — a detail no visitor consciously registers, but one the client, an architect herself, noticed on the first site visit and asked us to keep.

As an architect I was terrified someone would want to plasterboard over my columns. OBSIDIAN built around them like they were the client, not the obstacle.

Giulia B.
Owner, Stone Loft
