Casa Litoral

A rooftop penthouse over Lisbon's Alfama, built to hold the river light from noon until dusk.

Location
Lisbon
Completed
2023
Area
340 m²
Materials
Limestone, steel, teak
Duration
14 months
The brief

A single storey, added without looking added

The clients had bought the top floor of a nineteenth-century Alfama building for its view of the Tagus, and asked for a penthouse that wouldn't fight the rooftops around it. The existing structure couldn't take a heavy addition, so the new storey was framed in steel and clad in the same local limestone as the building below — from certain angles, it disappears into the block entirely.

Every room opens onto a wraparound terrace shaded by a pergola of reclaimed teak, oriented to block the worst of the afternoon sun while keeping the river fully in view from the kitchen island. A retractable glass wall lets the terrace and living room merge into a single space on the sixty or so evenings a year still enough for it.

Below the terrace, a cistern collects rainwater for the roof garden — a detail the clients didn't ask for, and the one they now mention first when showing people around.

Neighbours still ask which floor we added. That was the whole point — we didn't want a rooftop box, we wanted a home that had always somehow been there.

Inês & Tomás R.
Owners, Casa Litoral
