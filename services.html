01 / Process

How a project moves

01
Weeks 1–4

Site & brief

We spend days on site before drawing anything — sun path, wind, soil, sightlines. The brief is written together with you, not handed to you.

02
Weeks 5–14

Concept & material

A single strong concept, tested against the brief, budget and climate — alongside physical material samples, not renders alone.

03
Months 4–8

Design development

Every junction, fixture and finish resolved in detail. Engineering, lighting and landscape run in parallel, not after.

04
Months 8–10

Permitting

We manage local authorities and consultants directly, drawing on relationships built across fourteen countries.

05
Months 10–34

Construction

A dedicated site architect visits weekly, minimum. Our own workshops fabricate stone, timber and metalwork off-site.

06
Ongoing

Handover & aftercare

A personal curator remains on call for seasonal maintenance, adjustments and the inevitable question a year in.
