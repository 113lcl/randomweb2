Home/Philosophy

We design for what a house
feels like at seven in the morning.

Founded in 2011, OBSIDIAN is a private atelier working across fourteen countries — small enough to obsess over a single joint in the stone, large enough to run a project from the first sketch to the last key.

01
Villa Meridian
French Riviera, 2023

Every project begins with one question: what does a person truly need in this space? We remove everything that stands in the way of light, air and quiet — and keep only what matters.

01

Minimal form, maximum meaning

Every line is justified by light, climate and the way a person moves through the space. Nothing is drawn for the sake of a photograph.

02

Materials speak for themselves

Stone, glass and timber — unpainted, unimitated. Only the honest texture of time, sourced and finished by our own workshops.

03

Engineering stays invisible

Climate, light and security systems dissolve into the architecture rather than interrupt it. If you can see the wiring, we haven't finished.

“The best detail is the one you never notice, but feel every single day.” — Nicolas Verane, Founder
02 / Values

What we won't compromise on

01

Honesty of material

No veneer, no paint pretending to be stone. What you touch is what it is.

02

One team, start to end

The architect who draws the plan is on site the day the keys are handed over.

03

Radical privacy

Client identities, locations and plans never leave the studio without consent.

04

Slow when it matters

We take the time a joint, a view or a decision actually needs — not the time a schedule allows.

03 / Timeline

Thirteen years, one direction

2011

Studio founded in Geneva

Nicolas Verane opens a three-person office with a single commission: a lake house that took eleven months longer than planned.

2015

First stone workshop, Carrara

To stop relying on suppliers who "interpreted" our drawings, we opened our own cutting and finishing workshop in Italy.

2018

London studio opens

A second office to serve residences across the UK and Northern Europe, led by our climate-engineering team.

2021

50th residence completed

A concrete and glass house cut into an Aspen ridge, engineered to disappear from the valley below.

2024

Dubai studio, fourteen countries

Today the atelier works across four continents while keeping every project under thirty active commissions at once.
