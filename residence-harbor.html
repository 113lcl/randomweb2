Harbor Penthouse

A waterfront penthouse over the Copenhagen harbour, built around a private roof terrace and the changing light off the water.

Location
Copenhagen
Completed
2022
Area
295 m²
Materials
Oak, brass, glass
Duration
12 months
The brief

A rooftop built for one specific hour of the evening

The clients, longtime sailors, wanted a home that treated the harbour the way a boat treats the horizon — always visible, never intrusive. The top two floors of a former grain warehouse were combined into a single penthouse, with the roof terrace positioned specifically to catch the light bouncing off the water in the hour before sunset, a detail confirmed with a full year of sun-path photography before construction began.

Danish oak, oiled rather than lacquered, runs continuously from the entry through to the terrace decking, blurring the line between inside and out. Brass fittings throughout — door handles, tap ware, stair rail — were left unlacquered on purpose, so they'll tarnish and polish unevenly with daily use, the way the fittings on the clients' own boat always have.

A retractable glass canopy over the terrace extends the outdoor season by nearly two months on either end, engineered to fold flush against the parapet and disappear completely on the evenings it isn't needed.

We asked for a home that felt like the deck of our boat. Nobody else took that literally enough to spend a year photographing the sunset before drawing a single wall.

Mikkel & Sofie H.
Owners, Harbor Penthouse
