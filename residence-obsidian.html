Villa Obsidian

A residence cut into the mountainside, built to disappear into the rock it stands on.

Location
Swiss Alps
Completed
2024
Area
620 m²
Materials
Concrete, glass, steel
Duration
32 months
The brief

Live inside the mountain, not on top of it

The clients — a family who had skied this valley for three generations — asked for a house that wouldn't compete with the view. What we built instead reads as a single dark fissure in the slope, its roofline following the natural fall of the rock above it.

Eighty percent of the structure sits below the original grade, carved from reinforced concrete and waterproofed against the spring melt. The exposed face — glass from floor to ceiling — faces due south, pulling in low winter light while a two-metre stone overhang keeps the interior shaded through summer.

Black oxidised steel window frames match the exposed basalt found on site, quarried during excavation and reused as cladding for the entry wall. Nothing arrived from further than four hundred kilometres away.

Three winters in, the house still surprises us — the way the entry hall goes gold for eleven minutes every solstice morning. That wasn't in the brief. That was OBSIDIAN paying attention we didn't know to ask for.

Markus & Léa R.
Owners, Villa Obsidian
