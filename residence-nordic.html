Nordic Retreat

A ski-in cabin that reworks the traditional Swedish stuga without losing what made it work in the first place.

Location
Åre, Sweden
Completed
2020
Area
380 m²
Materials
Pine, granite, black steel
Duration
20 months
The brief

A stuga, still — just built for four families instead of one

The clients wanted a ski lodge that felt like the family cabins scattered across the valley — not a glass box dropped into the snow. Nordic Retreat keeps the steep pitched roof, dark timber and small deliberate windows of the traditional stuga, scaled up to sleep four generations at once without losing the low, hunched-against-the-weather profile that makes those cabins work.

Pine, sourced and milled within sixty kilometres of the site, clads the entire exterior and will silver with age the way every building in the valley eventually does — no stain, no sealant, just time. Local grey granite forms the base and the two fireplaces, chosen to match stone visibly quarried nearby rather than an imported alternative that would have stood out.

A boot room and ski store sit at the exact point the piste meets the property line, so no one ever tracks snow past the entry — a small piece of choreography that took longer to get right than any structural detail in the house.

My grandfather built the cabin this replaced with his own hands. OBSIDIAN is the only studio that understood we weren't asking them to improve on it — just to make it work for twenty more people.

Erik L.
Owner, Nordic Retreat
